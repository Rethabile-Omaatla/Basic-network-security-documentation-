# Common Network Security Threats

*Viruses and Ransomware – Malicious software that can damage systems, steal information or lock files until a payment is made.
* Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) Attacks – Attacks that overload a network or website with too much traffic, making it difficult for users to access it.
* Man-in-the-Middle Attacks – When attackers secretly intercept communication between two parties to steal or change information.
* Unauthorised Access – When someone gains access to a network or system without permission.
* Phishing Attacks – Fake emails or messages used to trick people into sharing information or clicking harmful links, which can allow attackers to enter a network.
